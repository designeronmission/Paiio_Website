What is Earned Wage Access?

Earned Wage Access (EWA) is a modern financial wellness tool that breaks the bi-weekly pay cycle. It allows you to draw a portion of the money you have already earned in your current pay period, bridging the gap between work done and payday.

It's Not a Loan. It's Your Money.

When you use our platform, you are simply accessing money you have already worked for. There are no interest charges, no credit checks, and no hidden fees for standard access. We partner directly with your employer to calculate your earned wages accurately and securely.

Everything you need to know about Paiio and Earned Wage Access.

Absolutely not. Paiio is not a credit product and we do not perform hard credit checks. Accessing your earned wages has zero impact on your credit score. We believe in transparent, stress-free financial wellness without the burden of credit inquiries.

Once you request an advance through the Paiio app, funds are typically transferred instantly to your linked bank account or debit card. Standard transfers may take 1–2 business days depending on your financial institution. You'll always see the estimated delivery time before confirming your request.

Your available balance is calculated based on your bank statement. When you securely link your bank account, we analyze your recent statement activity to determine your earned wages and establish your available balance. This gives you an accurate, real-time view of the funds you can access—no manual tracking or employer payroll integration required on your end.

Repayment is simple and flexible. Your active advances are automatically tracked in the Paiio app. You can choose to repay in full or make partial payments at any time before your scheduled payday. On payday, any outstanding balance is automatically deducted from your paycheck—no manual action required. You'll always have full visibility into your repayment schedule.
